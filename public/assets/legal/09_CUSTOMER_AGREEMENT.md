## 1. Services

### 1.1 Website Design and Development

Arumaldo will design and develop a custom website for Customer according to the selected package:

- **One-Page Professional**: A single-page, scroll-driven website as described in the proposal.
- **Multi-Page Corporate**: A multi-page website of up to six (6) pages as described in the proposal.
- **Service Commerce** (add-on): Integration of service listings, booking flows, or payment processing into the website, quoted separately per project and added to the above packages.

All websites are custom-designed. Arumaldo does not use off-the-shelf page builder templates.

### 1.2 Scope Defined by Intake Form

The scope of the website is defined by the content, specifications, and preferences submitted in the post-payment intake form. Work beyond the agreed scope — including additional pages, sections, or features not covered by the selected package — will be quoted separately.

### 1.3 Delivery Timeline

Arumaldo targets delivery of the initial preview within 7–14 business days of intake form submission, subject to timely receipt of all required content from Customer. Arumaldo is not liable for delays caused by Customer's failure to provide required materials or timely feedback.

## 3. Mandatory Hosting

All Arumaldo websites require active managed hosting through Arumaldo as a condition of the service. Hosting is mandatory and cannot be waived. A website built by Arumaldo will not be kept live or maintained on Arumaldo servers without an active hosting subscription.

### 3.2 Hosting Plans

Hosting begins at site launch and renews automatically until cancelled.

### 3.3 Hosting Services Included

Managed hosting includes:

- Uptime monitoring

Arumaldo makes commercially reasonable efforts to maintain uptime but does not guarantee 100% availability. Arumaldo is not liable for losses arising from hosting downtime beyond its reasonable control.

Customer may cancel hosting at any time. Upon cancellation:

- Arumaldo will provide a code export of the website files upon request; this may be quoted separately.

## 4. Change Requests

### 4.1 Included Change Rounds

Every website package includes two (2) rounds of revisions before launch at no additional cost. A "change round" is defined as a batch of revision requests submitted together in a single email or communication.

### 4.2 Change Round Scope

Each included change round applies to design adjustments, content corrections, and layout modifications within the original agreed scope. Change rounds do not cover: addition of new pages, major structural redesigns, or features not included in the original package.

### 4.3 Additional Change Requests

Changes requested after the two included rounds, or after site launch, are billed as follows:

- **Per Change Request**: $50.00 per request (billed as an add-on invoice)
- **Change Request Service — Monthly**: $99.00/month (5 change requests per month)
- **Change Request Service — Annual**: $999.00/year (5 change requests per month)

A "Change Request" is a single discrete task submitted via email (e.g., "update the phone number", "replace the hero image", "change the CTA button text").

### 4.4 Change Request Service

The Change Request Service is an optional recurring subscription billed separately from hosting. Customer may subscribe or cancel at any time. Unused change requests within a billing period do not roll over.

Service Commerce features are custom-scoped and priced per project. Commerce scope is defined in the proposal and confirmed at intake. Commerce features not in the original proposal are quoted separately.
